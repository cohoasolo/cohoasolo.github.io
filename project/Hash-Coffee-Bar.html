Brand Identity

Hash Coffee Bar

2022 Agency: Cillgold Agency
Client: HASH
Creative Director: LIÊM NG.
Project Manager: TRẦN NGỌC GIÀU
Sales Manager: CHOU CHOU
Designer: THÁI LÊ
Showcase: CHÍ CƯỜNG

In the Japanese neighborhood on Thái Văn Lung Street, HASH Coffee Bar is an ideal stop for those seeking a morning coffee, an afternoon glass of wine, or unique cocktails for an evening date. At HASH, you’ll find drinks to suit every time of the day, savor special light bites, and explore the cozy atmosphere of Japanese-inspired design.

HASH (#) represents the concept of elevated experiences and sharing them with like-minded souls. This is the primary goal of HASH Coffee Bar's founders and the creative seed that inspired Cillgold to design a distinctive brand identity for this establishment.
