Packaging

Herevia Distillery - Heritage Spirits

2022 Agency: Cillgold Agency
Client: Herevia Distillery
Creative Director: LIÊM NG.
Project Manager: TRẦN NGỌC GIÀU
Sales Manager: CHOU CHOU
Designer: THÁI LÊ
Showcase: ĐINH NGỮ, QUANG NGUYỄN
Motion Designer: QUANG NGUYỄN

As a craft distillery in Vietnam, Herevia Distillery pursues the philosophy of "SIMPLE SOPHISTICATION".

They approach the essence of traditional distillation techniques in their own unique way, offering products that satisfy both novice drinkers and passionate connoisseurs alike. This is how they convey the sophistication of their craft through a direct, simple, and pure tasting experience.

In this project, Cillgold collaborates with Herevia Distillery to design packaging for a collection of three types of rice-distilled spirits, following the ancient traditional methods of Vietnam, which Herevia Distillery has inherited, refined, and developed.
